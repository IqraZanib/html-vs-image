The Big Idea

  • A describing sentence tells us about a person — their name, age, family, what they like, and their school.
  • Every good sentence follows one simple pattern: Who/What + Action + Description.
  • Say the word, show a gesture, then use it in a full sentence.

🤝 For Struggling Students

Give sentence starters on the board: “My name ______.” and “I like ______.”

Challenge!

Use two target words in one sentence: “My family likes my school.”

✅ Check for Understanding
Which word means the people you live with?
→ family
“I am six years old.” Which word is this?
→ age
In the formula, what comes after Who/What?
→ action
