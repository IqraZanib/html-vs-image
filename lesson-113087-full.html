COMPREHENSION · WORD MEANINGS

My Talking Words

Describe yourself with 5 powerful words ✏️
Lesson ID 113087 Subject English Grade One Class 23 students Total 40 min
1
🎯 Objectives
2 min

Students will be able to use 5 target words — name, age, family, like, school — in descriptive sentences.APPLY

2
💡 Resources & Teacher Support
—
🧰 Resources
  • Whiteboard and markers
  • Flashcards of the 5 target words with simple icons (e.g. a thumbs up for 'like', a building for 'school')
🔑 Target Words
  • name · age · family · like · school
👩‍🏫 Teacher Note — teach meaning with gestures

Point to yourself for "name" · hold up fingers for "age" · hug your arms around yourself for "family" · give a big thumbs up for "like" · form a roof over your head with your hands for "school".

3
🚀 Introduction
5 min
👋 “Good morning, class! Today we are going to learn how to use some powerful new words in our writing!”
A short story: Once upon a time, a little girl named Pinky went to a big building to read and write. She felt so happy playing with her friends there! Can you guess where she was? Yes — at school!
Zainab has a big house where she lives with her mother, father, and baby brother. Who are the people Zainab lives with? What do we call them? (family!)
4
🧠 Explanation & Teaching
15 min
📋 Write the 5 target words & their meanings on the board
name
what people call you
age
how many years old you are
family
the people you live with
like
something that makes you happy
school
the place where you go to learn
🧩 Sentence Formula
WHO / WHAT
Who / What
+
ACTION
Action
+
DESCRIPTION
Description
Teacher
Model
“I want to use the word 'like'. I am thinking of something I enjoy doing. Who? I. Action? like. Description? to paint nice pictures. So, my sentence is: I like to paint nice pictures.”
Shared
Practice
“Let's build a sentence for 'school'. Bilal says he goes to school. Who? Bilal. Action? goes to. Description? a big school. Great job — Bilal goes to a big school.”
✅ Check for Understanding (CFU)
Q1. Which word means the people you live with?
Family
Q2. If I say “I am six years old,” which target word am I talking about?
Age
Q3. In our formula, what comes after “Who/What”?
Action
5
✍️ Practice
15 min

Mandatory Task: All students write original sentences for all 5 target words in their notebooks using the Who/What + Action + Description formula.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Note

Choose to work in Pairs (brainstorming) or Individually (focused writing) based on your class.

🌟 Teacher Support — Sample Reference Sentences
My name is Ali.
My age is six.
My family loves to play.
I like to eat apples.
My school has a big park.
🪜 Differentiation
🐣 For Struggling Students

Provide sentence starters on the board: “My name ______.” · “I like ______.”

🚀 For Advanced Students

Challenge them to use two target words in one sentence: “My family likes my school.”

6
🏁 Conclusion
3 min
👍👎 AFL Check — Thumbs Up / Thumbs Down
Listen to each sentence. Thumbs up if the target word is used correctly, thumbs down if it is wrong!
I eat my name for breakfast.
My family lives in my house.
I am age years old.
I like to play games.
I sleep in my school at night.
🎟️ Exit Ticket

Have 2–3 students read their favourite original sentence aloud to the class.

🏠 Closing & Homework

Draw a picture of your family and write one sentence about them using our formula. You all worked so hard today — well done, class!
